FDIR Copilot

An AI flight controller that finds the root cause of satellite anomalies and learns from every incident.

Who it's for

Small-sat operators: NewSpace startups and university CubeSat teams with 2 to 5 operators running many spacecraft and no 24/7 flight-controller bench.

The problem

Ops consoles alarm on limit violations. Limits fire on symptoms (battery temperature), not causes (a degrading solar array), and many faults never cross a limit at all. The result is hours of manual telemetry forensics per anomaly.

Why now

Thousands of small-sat launches a year, ops teams that aren't growing, and agents that can now reason over telemetry plus a causal model of the vehicle.

Route to first users

  • Free tier for university CubeSat teams (open-source telemetry importers: YAMCS, OpenMCT, CCSDS)
  • Pilot with 3 seed-stage NewSpace operators, paid per spacecraft per month
  • Channels: SmallSat Conference, LibreCube/CubeSat communities, launch-broker partners

How the agent works

Telemetry downlink → Aiven PostgreSQL
① Detect: orbit-aware residuals (eclipse ephemeris)
② Isolate: backtrack a system-dynamics causal graph
③ Recall: kNN over incident memory (Aiven PostgreSQL)
④ Fuse evidence → ⑤ recommend a recovery procedure + LLM briefing
Operator closes the incident → written back to memory → the next pass is smarter.
Deployed on Aiven Runtime.

Point of difference

Built on aerospace systems engineering (FDIR, FMEA, system dynamics), not a generic chatbot over logs. It explains why, with a traceable causal chain.
